# Yoruba AI Response Evaluation --- Evaluation Guidelines

## Purpose

These guidelines define a consistent method for evaluating synthetic
AI-generated Yoruba responses. The project demonstrates how a Yoruba
language specialist can assess linguistic and cultural quality for AI
systems.

## Evaluation principle

Evaluate the response against the user's prompt and the intended
meaning. Do not mark a response wrong merely because another valid
Yoruba wording is preferred.

## Evaluation dimensions

### 1. Meaning Accuracy

Does the response communicate the intended meaning? - 5 = fully
accurate - 4 = accurate with a minor nuance issue - 3 = partially
accurate - 2 = substantially incorrect - 1 = incorrect or unrelated

### 2. Grammar

Is the sentence grammatically acceptable? Consider word order, verb
construction, agreement, and relevant tense/aspect markers.

### 3. Naturalness

Would a competent Yoruba speaker consider the wording natural for the
stated context?

### 4. Diacritics & Orthography

Are Yoruba tone marks and underdots represented appropriately? Missing
tone marks may reduce this score when the task expects standard written
Yoruba. Informal contexts can be treated more flexibly.

### 5. Context

Does the response fit the situation described by the prompt?

### 6. Cultural Appropriateness

Does the response avoid culturally misleading or inappropriate language
and reflect the intended social context?

### 7. Instruction Following

Did the AI do exactly what the user asked, including requested
simplicity, language, format, or audience level?

## Overall score

Use a 1--5 score based on the total quality of the response, not a
simple mathematical average. A response with a serious meaning error
should normally receive a low overall score even if its spelling is
excellent.

## Error categories

Use one or more concise categories: - Meaning mismatch - Grammar -
Tense/aspect - Verb/meaning - Missing diacritics -
Orthography/diacritic - Naturalness - Child suitability - Cultural
context - Instruction following - None

## Quality-control rules

1.  Check the prompt before judging the response.
2.  Separate meaning errors from spelling/diacritic errors.
3.  Do not treat every alternative phrasing as an error.
4.  Preserve Yoruba tone marks and underdots in corrections where
    standard written Yoruba is intended.
5.  Use concise evaluator notes explaining the reason for the score.
6.  When uncertain about a regional or stylistic variation, flag it for
    linguistic review rather than presenting a preference as an absolute
    rule.
7.  Keep the dataset synthetic and free of private client data.

## Important project disclosure

All responses in the included dataset are synthetic portfolio examples.
They are not taken from a client's proprietary dataset and do not
represent work completed for a company.
